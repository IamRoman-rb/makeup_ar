## Where your data is stored

The app has no server of its own. Your account, profile, saved looks and profile photo are stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. We do not send them to any server of ours.

## What data we use and why

- Account: email, name, date of birth and a password stored in transformed form (PBKDF2 hash), never in plain text. Used to open your account on this phone and to calculate your age.
- Profile (optional): skin type, skin tone and makeup preferences. Used to personalise the app. [REVISAR: whether skin tone may be sensitive data under art. 2 of Law 25,326 and which consent it requires.]
- Profile photo (optional): you pick it from your gallery. We store a reduced copy without metadata (location, camera, date) in the app folder.
- Live camera: the image is processed on the phone to locate face points (eyes, lips, outline) and draw virtual makeup. No photos or videos are recorded, nothing is sent anywhere, and the image is not used to recognise who you are: no templates to identify people are created.
- Makeup guide: measures colours and positions of your face on the phone during the session. No image is stored.
- Reference and product photos (Studio): analysed on the phone; only numbers (colours and intensities) are kept. The photo is neither stored nor uploaded. Use photos of yourself or with the permission of the person shown.
- Saved looks and "My looks": the makeup recipes you save.

## Third parties that receive data

- VIP purchases: payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store; the app never sees your card details. To know whether you have VIP we use RevenueCat, Inc., which receives a random identifier of your account (not your email or name), purchase data and technical device data. [REVISAR: international data transfer (art. 12 Law 25,326) and provider agreement.]
- Fonts: the app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts servers the first time it uses them. Google receives your IP address and technical request data.
- Catalogue images and videos: if a look has an image or video hosted online, the hosting server receives your IP address when it is shown.
- System backup: if Android or iCloud backup is enabled, the operating system may include the app's data in your backup, under your Google or Apple account.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. The password is stored with a salted hash. The phone's database is not separately encrypted: someone who unlocks your phone or gains administrator (root) access could read it. Use a screen lock.
